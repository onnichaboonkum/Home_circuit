# Reel: มีประกันสุขภาพแล้ว… ทำไมยังต้องมีประกันโรคร้าย?

9:16 (1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, about 39 s) for Facebook / Instagram Reels and TikTok.

Output: `output/health_vs_critical_illness_reel_9x16.mp4` (+ `.th.srt` captions)

Render again:  `python3 insurance_reel/reel.py`   (stills: `--still 5,12,23`)

Requires ffmpeg, Python 3 with pycairo, pillow (with raqm), numpy, scipy.
Font: Kanit (SIL OFL, `fonts/`). Music and SFX are generated by the script, so they are original and royalty-free.

Text, timing and CTA lines are at the top of each `draw_*` function. The timeline constants are `LEFT`, `RIGHT`, `T_BOTH`,
`T_INSIGHT` and `T_CTA`. The narration captions are in `CAPTIONS`; their timing is ready for a recorded voice-over.

## Voice-over

The Thai voice is Microsoft Edge neural TTS, voice `th-TH-PremwadeeNeural`, generated by `generate_vo()`. It needs network
access to `speech.platform.bing.com`, and the free endpoint drops requests at random, so the script retries.
The generated lines and their word timings are cached in `audio/vo/` (`v0`–`v4` `.wav` / `.json`).
The scene timeline is built from those word timings (`build_timeline()`), so every pop-up appears on its spoken word.
The script text is `VO_SCRIPT`. Regenerate it with `--force-vo`, or use the cache with `--no-vo`.

To use a human or licensed voice, replace `audio/vo/vN.wav` with your recordings and run with `--no-vo`.
Keep the `.json` word timings, or delete them to fall back to fixed timings.
