# Reel: มีประกันสุขภาพแล้ว… ทำไมยังต้องมีประกันโรคร้าย?

9:16 (1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, 40 s) for Facebook / Instagram Reels and TikTok.

Output: `output/health_vs_critical_illness_reel_9x16.mp4` (+ `.th.srt` captions)

Render again:  `python3 insurance_reel/reel.py`   (stills: `--still 5,12,23`)

Requires ffmpeg, Python 3 with pycairo, pillow (with raqm), numpy, scipy.
Font: Kanit (SIL OFL, `fonts/`). Music and SFX are generated by the script, so they are original and royalty-free.

Text, timing and CTA lines are at the top of each `draw_*` function. The timeline constants are `LEFT`, `RIGHT`, `T_BOTH`,
`T_INSIGHT` and `T_CTA`. The narration captions are in `CAPTIONS`; their timing is ready for a recorded voice-over.
